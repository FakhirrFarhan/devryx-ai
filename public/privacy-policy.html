Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Devryx Labs ("we", "us") handles information when you use Devryx AI (the "Service") at devryxai.netlify.app and the associated Android app.

1. Information you provide

When you chat with Devryx AI, your messages and any images you attach are sent to our backend and forwarded to the underlying AI providers (Google Gemini for text/vision, and Hugging Face for image generation) in order to produce a response. We do not require an account, email address, or personal profile to use the Service.

2. Local storage

Conversation history, theme, and accent-color preferences are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so they persist between visits. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us for storage purposes — it is only sent to the AI provider at the moment you send a message.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Gemini API — processes chat and vision requests.
  • Hugging Face (FLUX model) — processes image-generation requests.
  • Google AdSense — may display ads on this site and use cookies or device identifiers to personalize them. You can control ad personalization via Google Ads Settings.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data it receives.

4. Cookies

We do not set first-party tracking cookies ourselves. Cookies you encounter on this site are generally set by Google AdSense for ad delivery and measurement.

5. Data retention

We do not maintain a persistent server-side database of your conversations. Chat history you see is stored in your own browser and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or using the app's "New chat" / export controls.

6. Children's privacy

Devryx AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

You can clear local chat history at any time from within the app, use ad-personalization controls provided by Google, or contact us to ask questions about this policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. Continued use of Devryx AI after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to devryxlabs@gmail.com or via the Contact page.
